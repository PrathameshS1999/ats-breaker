Keywords

Keywords are the skills, tools and certifications the job description names, plus short skill phrases from its requirement lists. Task wording such as "operate cloud environments" or "navigate ambiguity" isn't treated as a keyword.

Each keyword is weighted Required or Preferred by the heading it sits under, unless its sentence says otherwise ("is a plus", "must"). Then it's marked:

✓Matched◐Partial✕Missing

Matched means it appears in your experience. Partial means it's only in a skills list, or you name something related. Required keywords count double. When the job accepts one of several, such as "ACCA or CIMA", having one covers the others.

With Smart matching switched on, a line that says the same thing in other words, such as "took blood samples" for phlebotomy, also earns Partial. Never Matched: an ATS search still needs the exact words.

Fixes

Every fix is ranked by its estimated gain, such as ATS +6 or Parse +4.

Job title

Whether the job title, or most of its words, appears in your resume.

Experience and seniority

The years of dated experience we find, against the years the role asks for.

Education and certifications

Any degree or certification the job description names, against what your resume shows.

Recruiter readiness

6 rubric items
Needs workCompetitiveStrong
Measurable impact

How many bullets show a result with a number. Bullets are your experience and project points; skills, education and certification lists don't count.

Action-led bullets

How many bullets start with a clear verb.

Seniority fit

How your relevant experience compares with what the role asks for.

Concise bullets

How many bullets run over 35 words.

Skills shown in context

How many of the job's skills you mention appear in your experience, not only a skills list.

Clear sections

Whether you have Experience, Education and Skills headings.

Each item is rated Strong, Competitive or Needs work. Together they make a rubric score out of 100: 75 and above is Strong, 50 to 74 is Competitive, and below 50 is Needs work.
