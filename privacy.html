Free · no account · nothing stored

  • ✓No account. You don't sign up to use ATS_Breaker.
  • ✓Your resume and job description are read in your browser, on your device. They aren't uploaded anywhere.
  • ✓If you switch on Smart matching, your browser downloads a language model from Hugging Face and its code from jsDelivr, once. Those sites see an ordinary download request, as with any website, but nothing from your resume: the matching runs on your device.
  • ✓Your files are never stored or used for training.
  • ✓Your latest scores and the fixes you tick off are kept in this browser session only, so a rescan can show what changed. Your resume text is not kept.
